Snake,

SNAKE is simply one of my favorite arcade games. I already made a TUI version with linux that made it in the AUR. A lot of friends also wanted to play snake and asked me if I can make it available for Windows. I for myself thought that making it TUI for Windows wouldn't be the greatest idea. One reason for it was that my TUI SNAKE used my terminal styling which was missing in windows. This is why I made this SNAKE website.

// rules.md

Three rules,
one long tail.

The whole point of Snake is constraint. I've kept the rules simple — everything else comes from the geometry of your own decisions.

  1. 01
    Eat the dot.

    A single terracotta point appears somewhere on the 22×22 board. Reach it and your tail grows by one.

  2. 02
    Mind the walls.

    There is no wraparound. The board has edges, and edges are final.

  3. 03
    Avoid yourself.

    Your own tail is the most dangerous thing on the board.
